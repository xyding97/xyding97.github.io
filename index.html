About

I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Department of Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences at UCLA. My research focuses on stratosphere–troposphere coupling and its influence on weather extremes and subseasonal-to-seasonal (S2S) predictability. I combine atmospheric dynamics, observations, climate-model experiments, and AI-based atmospheric models to investigate the physical processes connecting large-scale circulation to regional weather and climate.
